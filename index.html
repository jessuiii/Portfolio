03ai · speech · ml-ops

        
FinEcho

        
Self-hosted call intelligence. Every sales call gets heard, translated and scored.

        
phone→drive→whisper→indictrans2→llama-3.2 ft→crm

        
I built this one end to end. Recordings travel from counsellors' phones to an ML server through an Android uploader. faster-whisper transcribes them, IndicTrans2 translates them, and a fine-tuned Llama 3.2 3B on vLLM summarises and scores every call on a 0 to 10 rubric before pushing it to the CRM. It handles Malayalam and Kannada in their own scripts and even writes "say this instead" coaching notes.
